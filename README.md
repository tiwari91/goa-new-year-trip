# Goa New Year Road Trip

A one-page planner for a four-day family road trip from Pune to the Goa border and back for New Year (30 Dec – 2 Jan): stay just across the border in Sindhudurg, where rooms cost far less than in Goa that week, and drive into North Goa for the night of 31 December.

- Day-by-day route with distances and drive times.
- Each night's stay (pick from seven Sindhudurg options) and each day's cost: fuel, tolls, stay, food.
- Editable budget; every stay keeps its own nightly rate.
- A "Before you leave" checklist.

Open `index.html` or the GitHub Pages site. Your shortlist, rates and ticks are saved only in your own browser.

Checked 16 Oct 2026. Hotel prices are rough estimates from old online listings, not New Year quotes; distances are approximate road figures; petrol is the latest published Pune price (7 Sep 2026).
