# Goa New Year Road Trip

A one-page planner for a five-day family road trip from Pune to the Goa border for New Year (29 Dec – 2 Jan): stay just across the border in Sindhudurg, where rooms cost far less than in Goa that week, and drive into North Goa for the night of 31 December.

- Day-by-day route with distances and drive times.
- Each night's stay (pick from seven Sindhudurg options) and each day's cost: fuel, tolls, stay, food.
- Editable budget; every stay keeps its own nightly rate.
- A "Before you leave" checklist.

Open `index.html` or the GitHub Pages site. Your shortlist, rates and ticks are saved only in your own browser.

Hotel prices are rough estimates from old online listings, not New Year quotes; distances and times are approximate.
