Goa New Year Road Trip

Stay just across the border in Sindhudurg, where rooms cost a fraction of Goa's New Year rates, and drive into North Goa for the night of 31 December.

Pune→Kolhapur→Amboli→Shiroda / Sawantwadi⇄Morjim · Arambol→Amboli→Kolhapur→Pune

Four days, 30 Dec to 2 Jan

About 965 km by car. New Year's Eve is a full day: four hours to the coast, a rest, then the night in Goa. The drive home is the long one. Each day shows where you sleep and what the day costs; change the numbers in the budget below.

Wed30Dec

Pune to Kolhapur

230 km · 4.5–5 h · NH48

Leave by 8 AM. Afternoon darshan at Mahalaxmi temple, Rankala lake at sunset. Early night in Kolhapur.

Thu31Dec

Over Amboli to the coast, then New Year in North Goa

155 km · 4 h to the stay, then 50–70 km round trip at night

Leave Kolhapur by 7:30 AM, Amboli ghat viewpoints on the way (the waterfalls are mostly dry by late December), lunch in Sawantwadi, check in by about 2 PM and rest. Leave at 5 PM for Morjim, Ashwem or Arambol; allow about 1 h each way tonight. Park early, watch the early fireworks, head back by about 12:30–1 AM. The driver does not drink.

Fri1Jan

Redi Ganpati, then Panjim and Old Goa

about 110 km round trip · 3 h

A slow start. Redi Ganpati and Redi beach in the morning, 10 minutes from the stay. Then Fontainhas, the Basilica of Bom Jesus and a late lunch; Mangueshi temple if there is energy. Or keep it quiet at Tiracol Fort and the beach.

Sat2Jan

Home via Amboli and Kolhapur

about 400 km · 8–8.5 h · NH48 from Kolhapur

Leave by 7 AM, back over Amboli ghat, lunch in Kolhapur, reach Pune by evening. The Belagavi route is about 470 km and an hour longer.

Picking a safe place

  • Book on MakeMyTrip, Goibibo or Booking.com so there is a record and a refund route.
  • Look for recent family reviews that mention gated parking, CCTV and staff at night.
  • Ask for a ground-floor room and power backup for the baby and your parents.
  • Prefer a main road or town over an isolated homestay; you return around 1 AM on New Year's night.
